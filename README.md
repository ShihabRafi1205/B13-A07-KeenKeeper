
# 🧡 KeenKeeper — Keep Your Friendships Alive

> **Never let a meaningful friendship fade away.**  
> KeenKeeper helps you track, nurture, and strengthen the relationships that matter most — one check-in at a time.

---

## 📖 What is KeenKeeper?

Life gets busy. We all have that friend we keep meaning to call but never do. **KeenKeeper** is a personal friendship tracker that reminds you to stay connected. Log your calls, texts, and video chats, set relationship goals, and see at a glance who needs your attention.

Whether it's your college roommate, your work buddy, or your childhood best friend — KeenKeeper helps you keep the bond alive.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. 👥 Smart Friend Tracking
Keep all your friends in one place with detailed profiles — including their photo, contact history, personal notes, relationship status, and custom tags. Instantly see who's **on-track**, who's **almost due**, and who's **overdue** for a catch-up.

### 2. ⚡ One-Click Quick Check-In
Reached out to a friend? Just hit **Call**, **Text**, or **Video** on their profile. KeenKeeper automatically logs the interaction to your Timeline with today's date — and shows a friendly toast notification to confirm. No manual note-taking needed.

### 3. 📊 Friendship Analytics & Timeline
See your entire interaction history in a beautiful Timeline view. Filter by Call, Text, or Video to understand your communication patterns. Then visit the **Analytics** page for a visual pie chart breakdown of how you stay in touch.
